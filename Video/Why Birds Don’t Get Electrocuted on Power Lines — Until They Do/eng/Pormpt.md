## AI continuity bible

- Model target: **Google Flow / Veo 3.1**.
- Generate each shot as **8 seconds, 16:9**, then use only the final range specified in the media map.
- Editorial palette: restrained cool-neutral daylight, natural contrast, no orange/teal blockbuster grade.
- Safe bird design: one generic small passerine, charcoal-brown and gray plumage, anatomically correct feet and wings, no named species claim.
- Safe-wire design: one uninterrupted, unbranded conductor; no second conductor, pole, transformer or grounded hardware within reach in safe-perch shots.
- Never generate readable text, logos, sparks, arcs, injury, smoke, carcasses, impossible wire geometry or a human near live equipment.
- Electricity is not literally visible. Any highlight is a clearly editorial scientific overlay.
- Preserve a clean first and last frame for match cuts. No morphing camera, identity drift or surprise objects.

### AI-01 — From neighborhood reality to the invisible system

**Final use:** 00:04–00:08; generate 8 s and trim.  
**Purpose:** Change scale and establish the system before introducing the bird.

```text
Create an 8-second, 16:9 editorial documentary transition that begins realistic
and becomes a clearly illustrative scientific visualization. No people.

TIMED ACTION
0.0-1.0 s — Begin on a quiet suburban street in neutral morning daylight. One
overhead power line crosses the upper third of the frame, matching a preceding real
shot. The camera begins a controlled lateral move.
1.0-2.3 s — Keep the physical street visible while a restrained semi-transparent
technical overlay traces the same line toward several ordinary homes. This is an
editorial overlay, not glowing electricity and not lightning.
2.3-3.3 s — A few windows illuminate one after another, suggesting a working
neighborhood grid without displaying data, numbers or text.
3.3-4.0 s — Tilt to a cloudy sky and arrive on one clean dark wire crossing from
lower left to upper right, with negative space for a match cut to real birds.
4.0-8.0 s — Hold that clean composition with only subtle natural cloud movement.
This is an unused stability buffer so the first four seconds can be cut cleanly.

CAMERA AND LOOK
Controlled documentary camera, 35 mm equivalent lens, natural perspective,
realistic wood, cable and building textures, restrained cool-neutral grade, no
dramatic storm, no commercial gloss.

FACTUAL GUARDRAIL
Electric current is not visible. The tracing line is an unmistakable editorial
visualization of system connection, not evidence of literal glowing energy.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No sparks, arcs, lightning, floating cables, impossible pole connections, extra
wires appearing, readable text, fake numbers, logos, watermarks, people, vehicles
moving through camera, rapid drone movement, geometry melting or CGI neon energy.
```

## Detailed briefs for footage the user will find

### REAL-01 — Neighborhood and electrical system

- **Story function:** Establish scale before the bird appears. The viewer must immediately understand that the wire belongs to an ordinary neighborhood power system, not an isolated nature shot.
- **What must be visible:** A quiet residential or small urban street; multiple houses or low buildings; at least one utility pole or a clearly traceable run of overhead distribution lines. The wire must be clearly visible from the first second.
- **Desired action:** Everyday life remains calm—subtle tree movement, a distant vehicle or lights turning on is acceptable—but the frame must not be visually busy. No person should address the camera.
- **Composition:** Landscape 16:9, 1080p or higher. Wide or medium-wide view. Place the strongest wire in the upper third, ideally running diagonally from lower-left toward upper-right so it can match `AI-01`. Leave clean sky around the line.
- **Camera:** Locked tripod, very slow lateral slider movement or slow controlled pan. No fast drone, handheld shake, aggressive zoom or whip pan.
- **Light and color:** Neutral morning, late afternoon or soft overcast daylight. Natural documentary color; avoid neon night scenes, dramatic lightning and orange/teal grading.
- **Duration needed:** Source clip should contain at least 6 clean continuous seconds. Final edit uses 4 seconds at `00:00–00:04`.
- **Best entry frame:** A stable wide view that can fade up from black without immediate subject motion.
- **Best exit frame:** The overhead wire is isolated against sky and occupies a clear diagonal that can match the first frame of `AI-01`.
- **Reject if:** The footage is vertical and cannot be cropped cleanly; the wire is barely visible; it only shows a high-voltage transmission tower with no neighborhood; there is a watermark, large logo, readable private information, unsafe human behavior, storm lightning or a dramatic electrical failure.
- **Search phrases:** `suburban street overhead power lines 4k`, `residential neighborhood utility lines video`, `distribution power lines street documentary footage`, `quiet neighborhood electrical poles stock video`.

### REAL-02 — The calm contradiction

- **Story function:** Show the real observable phenomenon before any explanation: birds calmly occupy a wire while nothing visibly happens.
- **What must be visible:** Prefer 3–12 small real birds perched on one clearly visible overhead utility line. At least one bird should turn, hop, flutter, arrive or leave so the shot does not feel like a still photograph.
- **Desired action:** Natural, low-intensity movement only. The important contrast is a working-looking wire and ordinary bird behavior, not spectacle.
- **Composition:** Landscape 16:9, 1080p or higher. Medium-wide or wide enough to show whole birds and the line. Keep sky as clean negative space. Do not choose a macro of feet or a frame dominated by one static bird.
- **Camera:** Locked or a very slow pan following the line. No repeated push-in toward the feet, no unstable handheld motion and no extreme telephoto shimmer.
- **Light and continuity:** Soft overcast or neutral daylight close to the ending sky of `AI-01` and the opening sky of `AI-02`. Avoid sunset silhouettes unless all adjacent shots are redesigned to match.
- **Duration needed:** At least 7 clean seconds in the source; final edit uses 5 seconds at `00:08–00:13`.
- **Best entry frame:** The wire angle and sky position closely match the last frame of `AI-01`.
- **Best exit frame:** One bird begins a small wing movement or looks into the direction from which the AI bird in `AI-02` will enter.
- **Factual limit:** Use this as generic real birds-on-wire B-roll. Do not claim that the specific recorded wire was live unless the source owner documents that fact.
- **Reject if:** Every bird is completely motionless; the clip only zooms into feet; birds are on a fence, cage or tree branch; the source is a rescue/accident video; there are visible injuries, logos, watermarks, heavy compression, fake sparks or an unclear license.
- **Search phrases:** `real birds perched on utility wire video`, `flock birds power line natural movement 4k`, `small birds on overhead wire documentary footage`, `birds landing leaving power line stock video`.

### REAL-03 — Geometry changes during takeoff

- **Story function:** Supply the real-motion bridge from safe stillness to the exception. The expanding wings make “the geometry changes” visible before the technical diagram appears.
- **What must be visible:** Several real birds begin perched on a clearly visible wire, then one or more take off in a continuous action. At least one full wing extension should be readable against the sky.
- **Desired action:** Perched → crouch or lean → wings open → feet leave the wire. Prefer one clean action rather than a chaotic flock already flying when the clip begins.
- **Composition:** Landscape 16:9, 1080p or higher. Medium-wide view showing the starting contact with the wire and enough sky for the wings. Keep the wire visible through the takeoff so the editor can freeze the final relationship.
- **Camera:** Locked, slow tracking or restrained natural slow motion. Preserve believable wing physics. Avoid speed ramps and dramatic crash zooms.
- **Light and continuity:** Neutral or softly overcast daylight compatible with the earlier bird shots. Background should be simple enough to become a silhouette-based diagram in `MG-04`.
- **Duration needed:** Source clip should contain 6–12 usable seconds around the takeoff. Final edit uses the strongest 4 seconds at `00:52–00:56`, plus an optional clean freeze at `00:59–01:01`.
- **Best entry frame:** Birds are still touching the wire for at least several frames before takeoff.
- **Best exit frame:** A clear expanded-wing silhouette with the wire still present, suitable for a match cut into the two contact diagrams.
- **Factual limit:** The clip demonstrates movement and changing contact geometry only. Never describe or edit it as if the recorded birds were electrocuted.
- **Reject if:** The clip begins after birds have already left; wings are hidden by buildings or foliage; the scene shows a predator attack, collision, panic, injury or dead bird; the source uses extreme slow motion, AI generation, a watermark or an unverifiable license.
- **Search phrases:** `birds taking off from power line slow motion`, `flock leaves overhead wire real footage`, `bird wings open takeoff utility wire 4k`, `birds perched then flying from wire documentary video`.
